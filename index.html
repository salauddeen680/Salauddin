FAQ

Who is Salauddin Saif?

Quick facts about Salauddin Saif and CCU Studios.

Who is Salauddin Saif?

Salauddin Saif (also known as Salauddin or Saif, @iamsaif.001 on Instagram) is an Indian comic creator, storyteller and the founder of CCU Studios, the Cosmic Cinematic Universe.

What is CCU Studios?

CCU Studios (Cosmic Cinematic Universe) is an original comic universe founded by Salauddin Saif. It includes ARHAAN 1985, ARYAN: The Beast, Orion: The Ascension, Michael: The Former Crown, TRIVEXA, Zenith: The Divine Shadow, AEK: The Shadow Entity, The Alliance and Legacy of Saif: Rise.

Which characters did Salauddin Saif create?

Arhaan, Aryan, Orion, Michael, Trivexa, Zenith, AEK and Saif, along with the team-up series The Alliance.

Is Salauddin Saif the same person as Salahuddin Ayyubi?

No. Salauddin Saif is a present-day comic creator and founder of CCU Studios. He is not the 12th-century Sultan Salahuddin Ayyubi (Saladin).
